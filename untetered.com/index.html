UnTETered

An MPE piano roll plugin for just intonation. Free your music from fixed tuning systems.

Dynamic Just Intonation

Conventional methods for writing music with MIDI are based on fixed tuning systems with a predefined number of notes. UnTETered enables you to break the rules of fixed tuning systems with dynamic just intonation. Start by writing a reference note and add notes at exact ratios of it. Then choose your next reference note. Dynamic just intonation lets you choose from an infinite set of notes while maintaining perfect harmonic consonance.

Out via MPE

UnTETered is simply a piano roll to write your music in. The sound is up to you. UnTETered is compatible with all instruments that support MPE.

Inside the host

UnTETered runs as a plugin in your DAW and follows its playback and loop control. Running UnTETered inside Ableton Live or Logic Pro lets you even control the DAW's playhead from within the plugin window.
