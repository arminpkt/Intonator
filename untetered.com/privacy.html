Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data is processed when you visit untetered.com, download or license the UnTETered plugin, contact us, or join our community, and what rights you have. We keep data collection to a minimum: we don't run ads, we don't use tracking or analytics, and we never sell your data.

2. Purchases, downloads and licenses (Moonbase)

UnTETered is sold and distributed through Moonbase (moonbase.sh), which acts as merchant of record. That means Moonbase, not us, is the seller in your purchase. Moonbase handles payment, invoicing and taxes and is itself responsible for the payment and order data it processes. We never see or store your payment card details. For details, see the privacy policy for buyers in the Moonbase store and the Moonbase Terms for Buyers.

When you download a trial, redeem a voucher or buy a license, our Moonbase account shows us the information we need to provide your license and support you. Typically that is your name, email address, country, which product or license you have, order or trial dates, and your license activations. For this data, Moonbase acts as our processor under a data processing agreement.

Purpose and legal basis: providing the software and license you asked for and supporting you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, performance of a contract), and preventing license abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest).

3. Visiting untetered.com

3.1 Hosting and server logs

Our website is hosted by Netlify, Inc. (USA). When you open the site, Netlify's servers automatically process technical data such as your IP address, the date and time of access, the page requested, and your browser type and operating system. This is necessary to deliver the website and keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not use this data to identify you.

3.2 Demo video (YouTube)

Our demo video is hosted on YouTube (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC). The video is not loaded until you click to play it. Until then, no data is sent to YouTube. Once you click, the video loads from youtube-nocookie.com, and YouTube processes your IP address and may store information on your device according to Google's privacy policy. Legal basis: your consent, given by clicking play (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

3.3 Cookies, fonts and tracking

We set no cookies, load no fonts or scripts from third-party servers, and use no analytics or tracking tools.

3.4 Links to other sites

Our site links to Discord, YouTube and the Moonbase store. Once you follow such a link, the privacy policy of that service applies.

4. License activation inside the plugin

When you activate UnTETered, and periodically afterwards to check that the license is still valid, the plugin connects to Moonbase's licensing servers. It transmits:

  • your license or trial activation token;
  • your computer's name as set in your operating system, so you can recognise your devices in your Moonbase account;
  • a device signature: a one-way hash computed from hardware identifiers of your computer (on macOS, the hardware UUID), which cannot be turned back into those identifiers;
  • the version of the licensing software;
  • and, as with any internet connection, your IP address.

This data is used only to activate and validate your license and to enforce the limit of 3 activations per license. You can view and release your activations yourself in your Moonbase account in the browser. The plugin does not send any audio, project data, usage statistics, system information or crash reports. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

5. Contacting us by email

If you email us (e.g. info@untetered.com), we process your email address, your name and whatever you write, in order to answer you. Our email is forwarded via Namecheap, Inc. (USA), stored in Gmail (Google), and sent via Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your message relates to a purchase or license, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (answering inquiries). We delete correspondence once it is no longer needed, unless we are legally required to keep it.

If you take part in our beta, we may email you about it (updates, feedback requests). You can ask us to stop at any time.

6. Discord community

We run a community server on Discord (Discord Inc., USA). Joining is optional. If you join, Discord processes your data under its own privacy policy. As moderators, we can see your username, your profile and the messages you post on our server. We use this only to run the community, handle bug reports and feature requests, and moderate (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

7. Who receives data

We share data only with the service providers named above (Moonbase, Netlify, Google, Namecheap, Brevo and Discord), as far as needed for the purposes described, or where we are legally required to. Some of these providers are located in the USA. Transfers there are based on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. How long we keep data

We keep license-related data as long as your license exists and as long as tax or commercial law requires. Moonbase applies its own retention rules. Emails are deleted once the matter is resolved and no retention obligation applies. Netlify keeps server logs only for a short period, for security purposes.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data (Art. 15), to have it corrected (Art. 16) or deleted (Art. 17), to restrict processing (Art. 18), to receive it in a portable format (Art. 20), and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21). Where you gave consent, you can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future.

To exercise these rights, just email info@untetered.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, for example the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (ldi.nrw.de) or the authority in your own country.

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling.

10. Changes

We will update this policy when our product or services change, for example when the full version launches. The current version is always available on this page.
